Chicken Schnitzel

Chicken Schnitzel

Ingredients

  • panko breadcrumbs
  • plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 large chicken breasts
  • olive oil

Method

  1. slice chicken horizontally in half, try to get even thickness pieces
  2. fill 3 bowls with flour, beaten eggs, and breadcrumbs. Coat chicken in flour, shaking off excess. Dip in egg mixture. Coat in breadcrumb mixture. Place on a plate. Repeat with remaining chicken
  3. Heat oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook chicken, in batches, for 4 to 5 minutes each side or until golden and cooked through. Transfer to a plate lined with paper towel to drain. Serve.
